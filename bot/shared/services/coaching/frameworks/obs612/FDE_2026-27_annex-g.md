| # | Stage | Indicators | Level 0 | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 | Observations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Before the lesson | Assigning and checking written work including formative assessments (e.g. quizzes, monthly/ end of unit tests) | Does not meet level 1 [ ] | Student notebooks show evidence of written work of the past 5 lessons assigned and checked by the teacher [ ] | Along with level 1, regular written feedback has been provided by the teacher. Fellow students/ mentors have been assigned to check assignments as well [ ] | Along with achieving level 2, formative (classroom) assessments have application-based questions that go beyond the simple copy/paste of the textbook [ ] | |
| 2 | During the lesson | A meticulous introduction/ opening provided based on specific Student Learning Outcomes (SLOs) | Does not meet level 1 [ ] | Teacher begins with the current SLO and links it broadly to the topic. [ ] | Teacher meets level 1 and links the SLOs to activities/tasks in class, and previous lessons or other subjects [ ] | Along with level 2, introduction is provided to the topic with an exciting hook which could include either a short story, interactive activity, a fun fact or a thought-provoking question to the students [ ] | |
| 3 | During the lesson | Effective teachers' command on the content/subject | Does not meet level 1 [ ] | The teacher can provide a lecture on the topic, however, she/he is unable to answer complicated questions from the students [ ] | The teacher meets level 1 and can answer questions from students, however, he/she is unable to rephrase content depending on the needs of the students [ ] | Teacher meets level 2, and is able to rephrase her/his content depending on the needs/ questions of the students. She/he can also discuss content that is not available in the textbooks [ ] | |
| 4 | During the lesson | Strong communication skills and delivery | Does not meet level 1 [ ] | Audible and coherent speech is delivered [ ] | Along with level 1, the teacher has a friendly and positive body language, and engages all students through movement in class [ ] | Along with level 2, the teacher uses engaging methods of delivery such as appropriate humour or short fun activities to grab the attention of the students [ ] | |
| 5 | During the lesson | Lesson is student-centric and activity-based | Does not meet level 1 [ ] | Lesson has one activity performed by the teacher and majority of the lesson is lecture-based. [ ] | Lesson includes one activity performed by students and activity is instructive and relevant to SLOs. [ ] | Lesson includes several different types of activities performed by students and activities are instructive and relevant to SLOs. These activities are a combination of group, pair or solo activities. [ ] | |
| 6 | During the lesson | Use of supporting material (Visual or digital aids) | Does not meet level 1 [ ] | Teacher effectively uses the black/ white board [ ] | Teacher uses a visual aid which is instructive and relevant to SLOs [ ] | Teacher uses several visual/digital aids of more than one type which are instructive and relevant to SLOs [ ] | |
| 7 | During the lesson | Connections to practical examples or knowledge content from the students' daily lives | Does not meet level 1 [ ] | Teacher gives 1 example of real-life application of SLO. The example is coherent with the students' prior knowledge and has a local context. [ ] | Teacher gives 2 or more examples of real life application of SLO. Common misconceptions are dispelled. [ ] | Teacher meets level 2 and asks students for examples of real life application scenarios [ ] | |
