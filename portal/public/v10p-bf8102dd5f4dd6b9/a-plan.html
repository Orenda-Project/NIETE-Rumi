Opening

0–5′
1

Show Pinky’s rhyme tree on p.105; say cat… hat slowly. I see, I think, I wonder. Do the ENDS sound the same? → “Yes, -at.” That is a rhyme. “Everyone: c-at, h-at. Same end! Which rhyme does your Nani sing at home?”

Explanation · I Do

5–13′
2

Say net… pet, stretching sounds; point to -et. Blend Cat: /k/ /a/ /t/. Show pot-dot (-ot) and map-cap (-ap). Cat ends -at: our word family. “Same ending, so they RHYME.”Think aloud: “I say net, then pet. The ends are the same, -et, so they rhyme. I join them with a line.”

I START, YOU FINISH
Chant tan… man. Write Cat, then sat; class blends and reads. Ask: does sat end like cat? Yes. Read “The cat sat on the mat.” “s-a-t, sat. The cat sat on the mat.”
