Teacher: Good morning class. Today we are going to learn about fractions. Who can tell me what a fraction is? Ayesha, you tell us.
Ayesha: A fraction is a part of a whole, miss.
Teacher: Very good, Ayesha. A fraction is a part of a whole. When we cut a roti into four equal pieces and take one piece, we have taken one fourth. We write it as one over four. The number on top is called the numerator and the number at the bottom is called the denominator. Everybody say numerator.
Class: Numerator.
Teacher: And the bottom number is the denominator. The denominator tells us into how many equal parts the whole is divided. The numerator tells us how many of those parts we have taken. Hamza, if I cut the roti into eight equal pieces and take three pieces, what fraction have I taken?
Hamza: Three over eight, miss.
Teacher: Correct. Three eighths. Now there are two kinds of fractions we will learn today. A proper fraction and an improper fraction. A proper fraction is a fraction where the numerator is smaller than the denominator. For example one over four, three over eight, two over five. In all of these the top number is smaller than the bottom number, so the value of the fraction is less than one whole. An improper fraction is a fraction where the numerator is bigger than the denominator, like seven over four or nine over five. Here the fraction is more than one whole. Bilal, is five over three proper or improper?
Bilal: Improper, miss, because five is bigger than three.
Teacher: Good. Now look at this one on the board: four over eight. Is it proper or improper? Sara?
Sara: Proper, miss, because four is smaller than eight.
Teacher: No, no. Four over eight is not a proper fraction, because four is half of eight. A proper fraction must be like one over four. Four over eight is not proper. Write that down. Four over eight is not a proper fraction.
Hamza: Miss, but four is smaller than eight.
Teacher: I have told you, four over eight is not a proper fraction. Let us move on. Now we will do an example together. Ayesha, come to the board. Draw a circle and divide it into six equal parts. Shade two parts. What fraction is shaded?
Ayesha: Two over six, miss.
Teacher: Yes. Two sixths. Is it proper or improper?
Ayesha: Proper, because two is smaller than six.
Teacher: Very good. Now everybody, in your copies, draw a rectangle and divide it into five equal parts. Shade three parts and write the fraction. Bilal, what did you write?
Bilal: Three over five.
Teacher: Correct. Three fifths, and it is a proper fraction. Now let us compare two fractions. Which is bigger, one over two or one over four? Think about the roti. If I cut it into two pieces, each piece is big. If I cut it into four pieces, each piece is small. So one over two is bigger than one over four. When the numerators are the same, the fraction with the smaller denominator is bigger. Sara, which is bigger, one over three or one over five?
Sara: One over three, miss.
Teacher: Correct. Now we will also learn about mixed numbers. An improper fraction like seven over four can be written as one whole and three over four. We call this a mixed number. Seven over four means seven pieces where each whole has four pieces. Four pieces make one whole, and three pieces are left, so it is one and three fourths. Hamza, write nine over five as a mixed number.
Hamza: One and four over five, miss.
Teacher: Good. Five pieces make one whole and four are left. Now for homework, write five proper fractions and five improper fractions, and draw a picture for two of them. Also write three over two as a mixed number. Any questions? Bilal?
Bilal: Miss, is one over one a proper fraction?
Teacher: One over one is equal to one whole, so it is not a proper fraction. A proper fraction is always less than one. Good question. That is all for today. Pack up your things.
